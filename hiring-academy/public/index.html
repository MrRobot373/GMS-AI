Programs

Four focused programs. Most students combine two or three of them; a few come for one and stay for the whole path.

Interview preparation

Practice the questions that are actually asked. We run mock interviews, shape answers around your real experience, and give you structured feedback so you walk in prepared rather than hoping.

Aptitude and reasoning

Quantitative, logical, and verbal reasoning drilled through timed sets. Aptitude tests reward speed and accuracy, and both come from repetition with the kind of problems you will see on the day.

Communication and workplace skills

Clear spoken English, professional written English, teamwork, and the habits that make the first weeks at a new job comfortable. These are the skills that turn an offer into a good fit.

Placement support

A resume that says what matters, a final round of mock interviews, and a working method for searching for roles: where to look, how to follow up, and how to compare two offers without guessing.

How the training works

Training here is hands-on from the first day. You are not an audience — you are a participant.

  • Small-group sessions. Every session is practical: mock interviews, timed aptitude sets, and structured practice of real workplace situations.
  • Feedback you can act on. After each session you know what to work on. We track your progress across the weeks, so improvement is visible to you and to us.
  • One student at a time for placement. Resume review, final mock interviews, and role search are handled individually, because your next job is not anyone else's.
  • Continued support after the offer. Negotiating the offer letter and settling into the first week are also part of the support — that is where most surprises happen.

Campus

Hiring Academy is based in Pune, Maharashtra. Training happens in a campus setting designed for focus: separate rooms for mock interviews and quiet practice, and a shared study area where group sessions and peer discussions happen.
