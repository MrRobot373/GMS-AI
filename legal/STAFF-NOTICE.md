# Template: privacy notice for your staff

> **For our customers.** Your organization decides how Aatmiq is used, so it's your job to tell your
> people what happens with their data (in India, a notice under the DPDP Act; in the EU, an
> information notice under the GDPR). This template describes what Aatmiq actually stores and who
> can see it. Replace the [BRACKETS], delete what doesn't apply, and have it checked by whoever
> handles privacy or employment law for you. It's a starting point, not legal advice.
>
> The text below is written to your staff.

---

# How [ORGANIZATION] uses Aatmiq, and your data

Effective date: [DATE]

[ORGANIZATION] gives you **Aatmiq**, an AI assistant for chat, documents, projects, tasks (Work AI)
and coding (Aatmiq Code). This notice explains what it stores about you, who can see it, and your
rights.

## It runs on our own servers

Aatmiq runs on servers [ORGANIZATION] controls ([in our office / data center / our cloud account]).
The AI models run there too. What you type, upload or create stays on those servers: it isn't sent
to an AI company, and it isn't used to train AI models.
[If you connect an external model service: "Some models are provided by [SERVICE]; when you choose
one of them, your messages are sent to [SERVICE] under its terms."]

[ORGANIZATION] is responsible for this data (the "data fiduciary" or "controller"). The company
that makes Aatmiq doesn't receive it.

## What Aatmiq stores about you

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Your account | Name, work email, job title, profile picture, password (stored only as a one-way hash), two-step sign-in setup, language and other settings, custom instructions |
| What you create | Your chats (all versions of each answer), documents you upload, projects and their sources, Work AI tasks with their steps and files, schedules, skills, Code workspaces |
| How much you use it | Tokens used per day, model and section, and requests for more tokens |
| Sign-ins and security | Signed-in devices (browser, IP address, when), and an activity log of security-relevant events: signing in, changing settings, downloading data, admin actions |
| Connected apps | If you connect your own accounts (for example Gmail or GitHub) for Work AI: the access granted, stored encrypted |

## Who can see what

- **Your chats are private.** Nobody else sees them in Aatmiq unless you share one to a project.
  Temporary chats aren't kept at all after 24 hours.
- **Documents** are private unless you share them with your workspace or add them to a project.
  **Projects** are seen by the people they're shared with. **Work AI tasks** are private unless you
  share them to a project.
- **Admins** can see your account details, how much you use, sign-ins and the activity log. They
  can't open your private chats in the app.
- **For legal and privacy requests**, an organization admin can download everything Aatmiq stores
  about a person, or erase a person and their data. [ORGANIZATION] does this only [when you ask, when
  the law requires it, or for an investigation under our [POLICY NAME]], and it is recorded in the
  activity log.
- **People who run the servers** ([our IT team / name]) can technically access the stored data, as
  with other company systems, and are bound by [POLICY / confidentiality obligations].
- [Only if "prompt logging" is switched on in Admin → Settings: "Chat conversations are recorded for
  compliance: [describe who reviews them and when]. Aatmiq shows a notice in the chat when this is on."]

## What the AI does with your work

- Answers come from AI models and can be wrong: check them before you rely on them.
- **Work AI** runs tasks in a private folder on the server, and asks before risky steps. When it
  searches the web, it does so through our own search service, without your identity. When it uses
  an app you connected, it acts with your access; you can disconnect at any time (Work AI →
  Connections).
- Your custom instructions and a project's instructions are given to the AI with your messages.

## How long it's kept

- Your content is kept until you delete it [or: chats are deleted automatically after [N] days
  without activity (Admin → Settings → Data & privacy)].
- When you delete something, it's removed from Aatmiq right away. Copies in the nightly backups are
  overwritten after [14] days.
- When you leave, [ORGANIZATION] [deactivates your account / erases your account and the content
  you made, after moving shared projects to a colleague] [within [N] days].
- Usage numbers and the activity log are kept [for [N] / as long as the system exists]; when an
  account is erased, its usage stays without the name and its activity entries under a pseudonym.

## Why we use your data

To give you the tools for your work, keep them secure, manage costs and comply with the law.
[India (DPDP Act): for employment purposes and other legitimate uses under section 7; EU/UK (GDPR):
legitimate interests and the performance of your employment contract; adjust for your country.]

## Your rights

- **See and download your data:** Settings → Your data → *Download my data* gives you a ZIP of
  everything Aatmiq stores about you.
- **Correct it:** change your profile in Settings; ask an admin for the rest.
- **Delete it:** delete chats, documents and tasks yourself; ask [CONTACT] to erase your account.
- Questions, requests or complaints: [PRIVACY CONTACT / GRIEVANCE OFFICER, email]. [India: you can
  also nominate someone to exercise your rights, and complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  EU/UK: you can complain to your data protection authority.]

## Changes

We'll tell you about material changes to this notice [by email / in Aatmiq].
